📚 CNF Rules

In Chomsky Normal Form, every production must follow the allowed forms below.

A → BC
A → a

Here, A, B and C are variables/non-terminals and a is a terminal.

The converter removes useless symbols, unit productions and ε-productions, removes left recursion, replaces terminals and finally converts long productions into binary productions.
