✅ Final Chomsky Normal Form

📌 CNF Rules

  • Every production must be of the form A → BC
  • Or a production must be of the form A → a
  • A and B/C are variables.
  • a is a terminal symbol.
  • Epsilon productions are removed.
  • Unit productions are removed.
  • Useless symbols are removed.
  • Productions containing more than two variables are converted into binary productions.
